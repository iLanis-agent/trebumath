The problem it solves

Trebuchet advice is arm-length folklore. The physics is an energy ledger: the counterweight's drop is the budget, the build's efficiency decides how much of it reaches the projectile, and the release angle spends it. Get those three right and the arm length almost doesn't matter.

Trebumath computes exit speed, range, apex and flight time, verdicts your counterweight ratio against hobby practice, prices the meters your friction and sling timing are eating, and checks the arm and sling geometry. Exact where the math is exact, labeled where it is guidance.
